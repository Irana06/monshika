## Yang baru di v1.3.0

**Catat transaksi dari screenshot bukti bayar**
- Selesai transfer atau bayar QRIS, buka halaman "Berhasil", screenshot atau ketuk Bagikan, lalu pilih **Catat di Monshika**. Form transaksi terbuka sudah terisi: nominal, toko atau penerima, tanggal dan jam, serta akunnya. Screenshot-nya ikut terlampir sebagai foto struk.
- Bisa untuk bank dan e-wallet apa pun yang punya halaman bukti bayar, termasuk transaksi yang tidak memunculkan notifikasi.
- Transfer ke akunmu sendiri (misalnya ShopeePay ke BSI) dikenali sebagai transfer, kalau akun tujuannya ada di Monshika. Biaya admin ikut terisi.
- Tombol **Foto struk** di transaksi baru yang masih kosong juga ikut mengisi otomatis.
- Hasil bacaan selalu muncul di form untuk dicek dulu, tidak pernah langsung tersimpan.
- Teks dibaca langsung di HP oleh Google Play Services, tanpa dikirim ke server mana pun.

Saat pertama dipakai, pembaca teks mungkin perlu diunduh dulu oleh Google Play Services. Kalau muncul pesan "masih diunduh", coba lagi sebentar lagi.

## What's new in v1.3.0

- **Log a transaction from a payment screenshot.** After a transfer or QRIS payment, open the "Success" page, take a screenshot or tap Share, and choose **Log in Monshika**. The transaction form opens already filled in: amount, store or recipient, date and time, and the account. The screenshot is attached as the receipt photo.
- Works with any bank or e-wallet that shows a receipt page, including payments that never send a notification.
- Transfers to your own account (for example ShopeePay to BSI) are recognised as transfers when the destination account exists in Monshika, admin fee included.
- **Receipt photo** on a new, empty transaction fills the form the same way.
- Results always appear in the form for you to check; nothing is saved automatically.
- Text is read on your phone by Google Play Services and never sent to any server.

On first use, Google Play Services may still need to download the text reader. If you see a "still downloading" message, try again shortly.
